About

I'm a Computer Science grad from UNC Charlotte (IT concentration, Statistics minor), now in the first year of my M.S. in Information Technology with a concentration in Information Security & Privacy.

My first real enterprise exposure was interning with Mecklenburg County, managing accounts and permissions in Active Directory and Entra ID, building VMs in Azure and vSphere, and helping migrate servers to the cloud. That's where identity clicked for me: almost every security problem eventually comes back to an account that has more access than it should.

Since then I've been building the tools and labs to get good at it: an Active Directory lab I can break and fix, a PowerShell access-review audit, and a phishing analyzer for the SOC side. Right now I'm studying for Security+ and looking for IAM, SOC, and IT roles in Charlotte or remote.
